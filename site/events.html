Training topics

This topic aims to strengthen the competitiveness of MSMEs by improving product quality, compliance with relevant standards, packaging and labelling practices, post-harvest management, and value addition. This topic also seeks to strengthen the capacities of chambers of commerce and other partner institutions to ensure the long-term sustainability and scalability of business support services.

Training modules

  • Post-Harvest Handling, Storage and Loss Reduction
  • Product Quality and Standards Readiness
  • Value Addition
  • Packaging, labelling and brand presentation
  • Export requirements

This topic aims to equip MSMEs to access markets by building practical export and trade capabilities. It covers export readiness and basic compliance, the documentation and customs procedures required to move goods legally and efficiently, and the logistics skills needed to manage courier and fulfillment processes. It also strengthens marketing and negotiation for buyer engagement, alongside e-commerce and digital export skills to help firms reach customers through online channels.

Training modules

  • Can I export?
  • Where should I export?
  • Who will buy my products?
  • How should I structure the deal?
  • How do I deliver the shipment?

This topic aims to improve MSMEs’ ability to manage, formalize, and finance their operations. It builds core business foundations and business model development, strengthens bookkeeping, cashflow and financial management, and supports business formalization so firms can engage more easily with financial institutions and larger buyers. It also emphasizes risk management and resilience so businesses can withstand shocks and operate sustainably.

Training modules

  • Business Foundations and Business Model Development
  • Bookkeeping and Financial Management (Finance for non Financial Professionals)
  • Business Formalization and Regulatory Compliance
  • Access to Finance and Investment Readiness
  • Risk Management and Enterprise Resilience
