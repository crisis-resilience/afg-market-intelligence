About MSME Support Centre

The Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME) Support Centre serves as a one-stop platform to strengthen export readiness, value addition, and enterprise resilience for Afghan MSMEs, with a particular focus on women-led and rural businesses. Its mandate is structured around three focus areas, each designed around the needs of Afghan MSMEs and supported by an Afghan-led governance and partnership model:

  1. 1

    Improve operational efficiency

    Helping enterprises meet product quality and standards requirements, upgrade packaging and labeling to be market- and brand-ready, and reduce post-harvest losses through better storage and handling.
  2. 2

    Help to sell abroad

    Equipping MSMEs to access markets by building practical export and trade capabilities through B2B meetings, cohort-based support, capacity building for chambers, and the use of a market intelligence tool to guide decisions.
  3. 3

    Help MSMEs manage finance

    Improving MSMEs’ ability to manage, formalize, and finance their operations by moving viable firms toward financing opportunities and continued capacity building for chambers to provide ongoing support.
